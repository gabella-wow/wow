Build

On the beta tier list Subtlety is C for dungeons: it is a PvP tree. At level 30 it gets Hemorrhage, a strike with no need to stand behind the target that makes Rupture deal 15% more damage for 15 seconds. It becomes your main strike, except for Ambush from Stealth.

TipFor dungeons the beta tier list points to Assassination or Combat. A Subtlety Backstab build needs both Hemorrhage and Puncturing Wounds, and at level 30 there are not enough points for both (Wowhead).

Wowhead calls Opportunity, Initiative, Premeditation, Serrated Blades and Hemorrhage mandatory and the rest a matter of taste. Icy Veins adds Improved Ambush and Ghostly Strike, Wowhead adds Elusiveness, Dirty Tricks and Master of Deception. With Legacy points both take Preparation: it resets your cooldowns, which means a second Vanish.

Leveling 1→60

Which spec to level

Level Combat. Mobalytics and Icy Veins recommend it: Riposte and defensive talents keep you in the fight without rest, and from level 30 Blade Flurry hits two enemies at once. In dungeons, Combat is also the most reliable spec.

Warcraft Tavern doesn’t pick one tree: it advises against Assassination as a start (strong once you have Mutilate, but fragile), and Subtlety is good for an ambush opener but weaker in dungeons. The leveling tier list gives Rogue a B for Assassination or Combat.

SpecWhen to take itDownside
CombatSolo and dungeons, the whole way to 60Weak against several enemies until level 30 (Blade Flurry)
AssassinationFrom level 30, once you have Mutilate and daggersLess survivability

Who to group with

There is little area damage, but a lot of control. From level 30, Blade Flurry covers pairs of enemies.

Build the group in the group builder: up to 5, 10, 20 or 40 players, with a link and a text for looking for a group.

Rotation

The level 60 rotation will come after the Nov 5 launch. For now this is the level 30 order of actions from the beta, as given by Icy Veins and Wowhead.

  1. 1
    Opening

    Garrote or Ambush from Stealth.

  2. 2
    Hemorrhage

    Your main strike.

  3. 3
    Slice and Dice

    Keep it up.

  4. 4
    Rupture

    On a sturdy target that will live through its full duration.

  5. 5
    Eviscerate

    Spend spare combo points.

  6. 6
    An enemy turns to you

    Evasion or Vanish (Icy Veins).

Questions

How do I copy the build?

In the Talents section, press “Make your own build”, place the points and press “Copy build”: the points are kept in the link. The button at the top of the page shares a link to this cheat sheet.

What is the “Leveling” tab in the talents for?

“General” is the level 30 point layout from the beta. “Leveling” is the order of points from level 10 to 60; levels 31–60 come from the class leveling guide, and we will check them against the game after launch.

What about the Legacy points?

The Legacy perk Talented (up to 5 ranks) unlocks talent points earlier than normal, by up to 5 levels. Where to spend them is in the Talents section when the sources name it. All perks are in the Legacy guide.
